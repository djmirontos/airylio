Frequently Asked Questions

Airylio combines live traffic data from Google Routes, real-time weather conditions, and your historical trip data to calculate your exact departure time. It factors in buffer time based on how confident the prediction is.

Airylio is optimized for Philippine traffic patterns, road networks, and weather. While the app works internationally, recommendations are most accurate within the Philippines.
