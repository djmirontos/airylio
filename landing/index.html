Common questions

Yes, Airylio is completely free to download and use. We make our money through premium features and partnerships, not by charging users.

Airylio supports all cities and provinces in the Philippines with Google Routes coverage. This includes major urban centers like Metro Manila, Cebu, Davao, and surrounding areas.

Our recommendations achieve 90%+ accuracy by combining real-time traffic data, historical patterns, weather conditions, and route-specific factors. The more you use Airylio, the better it learns your patterns.

Airylio requires internet for live traffic and weather data. However, we cache recent routes so you can view past trip data offline.

Weather significantly impacts commute times, especially during rainy season. We add buffer time during heavy rain or storms to account for slower traffic and reduced visibility.

Your privacy is important to us. We only collect anonymized location data to calculate routes and improve our recommendations. We never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
